# The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office

An auto-reply that says the quiet part out loud: thank you, I am away, and your email will be answered once the 400 unread messages I am currently pretending do not exist have been dealt with. Polite on the surface, honest underneath — the way a real inbox actually feels.

## Entry

`main.py` writes one sentence (137 bytes) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Thank you for your email — I am away and will reply on my return, after the 400 unread messages I am currently pretending do not exist.

The honesty is the inbox: 400 unread messages being pretended away. The politeness is the thank-you and the promise to reply. Standard library only, no network, no third-party packages, deterministic.
